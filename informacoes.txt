# CORE TEACHING PHILOSOPHY

## Our Proposal

Each class is planned to balance:

* Welcoming environment
* Learning
* Technical development
* Individual evolution
* Confidence
* Connection
* Musicality

At the beginning of the class, we create an environment where students feel comfortable meeting new people, activating their bodies and getting into the atmosphere of the class.

During the main part of the class, we encourage concentration and teach the fundamentals of Brazilian Zouk in a clear, organized and progressive way, respecting each person's pace.

At the end, we want students to recognize their own evolution, feel more confident in their bodies and leave wanting to return the following week.

---

# HOW WE TEACH

## Learning with clarity. Dancing with confidence.

Students can expect:

* A respectful and welcoming environment from the first class.
* Clear and objective explanations for beginners.
* Positive feedback and individual attention.
* Encouragement throughout the learning process.
* Gradual evolution respecting individual and group pace.
* Development of technique, connection and musicality.
* The satisfaction of recognizing their own progress.
* An opportunity to meet new people and become part of the Zouk community.

# OUR PHILOSOPHY

We believe learning Zouk is a process built step by step.

As teachers, we aim to explain the fundamentals in a simple and organized way, providing clear guidance and feedback so that each student can develop confidence in their own learning process.

We value a classroom where people feel respected, supported and motivated to continue evolving, regardless of age or previous dance experience.
